Dealer operations · Service experience

Service marketplace

One place to discover, compare and manage dealer services.

A unified service marketplace designed to help dealer teams find the right operational services, understand value, and move from discovery to action with less effort.

View the solution View impact

Project overview

Role

Product Designer

Product

Service marketplace

Users

Dealer teams

Scope

End-to-end UX

My contribution

User flows, information architecture, interaction design, prototypes, and usability validation.

The challenge

Finding a service should not feel like finding a file.

Dealer teams had to navigate disconnected information, understand different service offerings, and rely on multiple channels before deciding what to use.

Opportunity

Turn fragmented service information into a clear, searchable, action-oriented marketplace.

What I designed

A marketplace built around how dealer teams work.

01

Discover

Clear categories and search help teams find relevant services without navigating multiple systems.

02

Understand

Service information is structured around benefits, requirements, and practical next steps.

03

Take action

Clear calls to action help users move from service discovery to the next operational step.

Selected solution

Find the right service. Book with confidence.

The marketplace brings service discovery, package comparison and booking into one simple journey for dealership customers.

Design impact

A clearer path from service need to action.

Reduced discovery effort

A central marketplace reduces the need to search across disconnected channels.

More informed choices

Consistent service information helps teams understand options before acting.

A scalable foundation

A consistent marketplace model can support additional services over time.

Design takeaway

Enterprise marketplaces work best when they organise complexity around the user's next decision—not the organisation's internal structure.
