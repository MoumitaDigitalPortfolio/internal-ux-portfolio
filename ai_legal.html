Internal case study / 2026

Making complex legal review easier to act on.

A focused redesign that helped legal teams move from document-heavy review to clearer, evidence-backed decisions.

My contribution

End-to-end product design

Role

Product Designer

Team

Product & Engineering

Platform

Enterprise web

Scope

Core workflow

01

Challenge

Dense documents made important risks difficult to identify quickly.

02

Design focus

Surface relevant information without removing legal control.

03

Outcome

A clearer review flow built around evidence and action.

The opportunity

Reduce the effort, not the accountability.

Legal professionals were spending significant time scanning lengthy documents, comparing clauses, and deciding which findings required attention.

Design principle

AI reads. Legal professionals verify and decide.

The solution

A review experience designed around decisions.

The workflow brought risk, supporting evidence, confidence, and next actions into one focused workspace.

01

Explain the finding

Every AI flag was paired with source evidence and a clear reason.

02

Show confidence clearly

Simple confidence levels helped users understand when to review.

03

Keep humans in control

Accept, edit, and reject actions preserved ownership and accountability.

Project impact

What changed after the redesign.

Review

Clearer priorities

Important findings became easier to identify and evaluate.

Decision-making

Less manual scanning

Users could focus more time on validation and action.

Trust

More visible control

Evidence and inline actions supported accountable use of AI.

Key takeaway

In complex enterprise systems, better UX is often about making the next decision easier—not showing more information.
