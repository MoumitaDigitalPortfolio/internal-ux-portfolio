Workflow

The challenge

The work started long before writing.

Teams spent significant time searching across disconnected sources, comparing information, and consulting experts before they could begin creating a document.

Design principle

AI accelerates the work.
People remain accountable for the decision.

Design approach

AI handles the first pass. People keep the final say.

01

Find

Relevant information is brought together from trusted enterprise knowledge sources.

02

Generate

AI creates a structured first draft using the brief, context, and selected information.

03

Review

Users verify, edit, and approve the content before publishing the final document.

Selected solution

From a simple brief to a ready-to-review document.

The workflow helps users define their requirements, guide the AI and review the generated document with confidence.

Design principle

AI helps users create the first draft. Users stay in control of the final document.

Outcome

Less time searching. More time improving the document.

User value

Faster starting point

Relevant knowledge is surfaced before users begin creating content.

Workflow value

Less repeated work

Existing enterprise knowledge can be reused instead of recreated.

Trust value

Human-led approval

Users can review, edit, verify, and approve the final output.

Design learning

In enterprise AI, speed creates value only when people can understand, verify, and remain responsible for the result.
